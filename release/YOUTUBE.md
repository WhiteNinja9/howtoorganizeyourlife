## Getting views (honest version)

Nobody can promise "massive views". What reliably moves a small channel:
- **Shorts.** Cut 3 vertical clips (the habit chain 0:40–0:50, the prayer timeline 0:50–1:02, the
  "wasting time" quote 1:08–1:15) and link them to this video. Shorts are where new channels get discovered.
- **Consistency.** One video a week in the same style beats one perfect video.
- **The first hour.** Share it with people who will actually watch to the end; watch time and replies to
  the pinned comment are the signals YouTube uses to show it to more people.
- **A series.** Same look, same wordmark, one topic each (Fajr, the tongue, the heart, Jumuʿah…), so
  viewers who like one binge the rest.
